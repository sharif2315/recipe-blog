Boulangerie Series

Heirloom Sourdough Boule

72 hrs (Total Time)

Advanced Difficulty

Makes 1 Large Loaf

Jump to Instructions

"A three-day journey of fermentation resulting in a deep, complex crumb and a blistered, golden crust. This boule celebrates the slow, intentional ritual of traditional breadmaking."

Ingredients

500g Stone-ground Bread Flour

100g Active Sourdough Starter

Rice Flour (for dusting)

375g Filtered Water (Room Temp)

10g Sea Salt

Preparation

Autolyse

Combine the bread flour and water in a large ceramic bowl until no dry spots remain. Cover with a damp linen cloth and let the mixture rest for at least 1 hour. This allows the flour to fully hydrate and gluten development to begin naturally.
